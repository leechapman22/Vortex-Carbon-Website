Privacy policy

How Vortex Carbon Ltd uses the details you give us. Last updated 24 November 2026.

Who we are

Vortex Carbon Ltd, Hunstanton, Norfolk. We are responsible for the personal details you give us. Contact us at lee@vortexcarbon.co.uk or on 07568 308 119.

What we collect

When you use the booking form we collect the job you need, how soon, your address or park and pitch, postcode, name, phone number and any details you add. If you call, text, WhatsApp or email us, we keep the details you send.

How we use it

Only to reply to you, arrange and carry out the job, send your certificate or invoice, and keep the records the law requires of a gas engineer and a limited company. We do not sell your details or use them for marketing.

Who else handles it

Booking requests are sent through Netlify, which hosts this website, and arrive in our Microsoft 365 email. Card payments are taken by Stripe on its own secure pages. These companies only process the details to provide their service to us.

How long we keep it

We keep your details for as long as we need them for the job and to meet our legal, gas safety and accounting duties.

Cookies and visit counts

This website does not use cookies. We count visits with GoatCounter, which does not use cookies or collect personal details.

Your rights

You can ask to see the details we hold about you, have them corrected or deleted, or object to how we use them. Email lee@vortexcarbon.co.uk. If you are unhappy with how we handle your details, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
