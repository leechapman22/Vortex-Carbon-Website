Common questions

What is the difference between Home Safe and Home Guard?

Home Safe covers the yearly gas safety certificate, boiler service and pre-season check. Home Guard adds the end-of-season drain-down, priority call-outs at a reduced charge, key holding, letting agent liaison and photos with every report.

How much does it cost?

Home Safe is £130 a year and Home Guard is £195 a year.

Are repairs included?

No. Repairs and parts are priced separately, before any work starts.
