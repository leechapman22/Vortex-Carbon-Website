Who needs one

If you let your caravan or lodge to paying guests, the law requires a gas safety check every 12 months by a Gas Safe registered engineer, and you must keep the certificate.

Many parks and insurers also ask owners for a current gas safety certificate, whether the caravan is let or not.

Common questions

How much is a caravan gas safety certificate?

A gas test and service for a static caravan or lodge is £80, including the gas safety certificate.

How often does a static caravan need a gas safety check?

Every 12 months if the caravan is let. For private owners a yearly check is still recommended, and many parks ask for it.

How long does it take?

Allow about an hour.
